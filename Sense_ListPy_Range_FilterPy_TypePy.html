Selects the sweep mode for a particular half decade.

Parameters:

  • filter_type –

    • NORMal: Measurement based on spectrum analyzer data.

    • FFT: Measurement based on spectrum analyzer data. Kept for compatibility to R&S FSV.

    • IQFFt: Measurement based on I/Q data.

  • rangePy – optional repeated capability selector. Default value: Ix1 (settable in the interface ‘Range’)
