Turns the activity tracking of utilizations on and off.

Parameters:

  • idn – ID number of the utilization activity. To find out the ID of the utilization, you have to query the complete utilization data. For more information, see method RsFsw.diagnostic.hums.utilization.get() .

  • state – ON | OFF | 1 | 0

    • ON | 1: Tracking activity = on

    • OFF | 0: Tracking activity = off

Returns:
