Commands in total: 1

# [SENSe]:CAPTure:OVERsampling
# get:
value: enums.OversampleFactor = driver.applications.k149Uwb.sense.capture.oversampling.get()
# set:
driver.applications.k149Uwb.sense.capture.oversampling.set(ov_factor = enums.OversampleFactor.OV10)

Enables or disables a capture oversampling factor.

Parameters:

ov_factor – 1 | 2

  • 1: Capture oversampling is disabled (capture sample rate = system sample rate)

  • 2: Oversampling factor 2 is enabled (capture sampling rate = 2``*system`` sample rate)
