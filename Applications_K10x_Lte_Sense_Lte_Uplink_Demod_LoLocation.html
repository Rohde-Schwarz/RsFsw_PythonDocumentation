Selects the location of the local oscillator in a system with contiguous aggregated carriers.

Parameters:

location –

  • CACB: LO is at the center of the aggregated channel bandwidth.

  • CCC: One LO is at the center of each component carrier.

  • USER: One LO is used for all component carriers. The frequency is not necessarily at the center of the aggregated channel bandwidth. You can define the LO frequency with .
