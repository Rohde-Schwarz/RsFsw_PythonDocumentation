// Set:
driver.Applications.K18_AmplifierEt.Configure.Dpd.Method.Set(DpdMethodEnum.GENerator);

This command selects the method with which the application determines the DPD. Prerequisites for this command

  • Turn on polynomial DPD (CONFigure:DDPD[:STATe]) .

  • GENerator: Signal generator applies the DPD parameters calculated by the amplifier application to the generated RF signal in real-time.Option R&S SMW-K541 is required on the generator for this method.

  • WFILe: Signal generator applies the DPD to the generated RF signal through a waveform file.No additional equipment is required on the signal generator for this method.Use CONFigure:DPD:FILE:GENerate to actually generate the DPD and transfer it to the generator.
