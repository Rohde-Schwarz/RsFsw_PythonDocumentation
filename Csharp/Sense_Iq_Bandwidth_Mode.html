Defines how the resolution bandwidth is determined.

mode AUTO | MANual | FFT

  • AUTO: (Default) The RBW is determined automatically depending on the sample rate and record length.

  • MANual: The user-defined RBW is used and the (FFT) window length (and possibly the sample rate) are adapted accordingly. The RBW is defined using the [SENSe:]IQ:BWIDth:RESolution command.

  • FFT: The RBW is determined by the FFT parameters.
