Activates or deactivates additional filters applied after demodulation to filter out unwanted signals, or correct pre-emphasized input signals.

type NONE | LP01 | LP1 | LP5 | LP10 | LP25

  • NONE: No video filter applied

  • LP01: Low pass filter 0.1 % bandwidth

  • LP1: Low pass filter1 % bandwidth

  • LP5: Low pass filter 5 % bandwidth

  • LP10: Low pass filter 10 % bandwidth

  • LP25: Low pass filter 25 % bandwidth
