interpolation WIENer | LINear

  • WIENer: The unused subcarriers of the channel estimation fields (1xHE-LTF, 2xHE-LTF) are determined by Wiener interpolation. The estimated channel is smoothed. The Wiener interpolation overwrites the used subcarrier sampling points.In case all subcarriers are used (4xHE-LTF) , sampling points for all subcarriers are available. The channel is smoothed.

  • LINear: The unused subcarriers of the channel estimation fields (1xHE-LTF, 2xHE-LTF) are determined by linear interpolation. Linear interpolation preserves the used subcarrier sampling points.In case all subcarriers are used (4xHE-LTF) , the available sampling points for all subcarriers are preserved, and no interpolation is applied.
