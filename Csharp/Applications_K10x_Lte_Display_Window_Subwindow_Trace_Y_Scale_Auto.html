driver.Applications.K10x_Lte.Display.Window.Subwindow.Trace.Y.Scale.Auto.Set(AutoScaleModeEnum.ALL);

Automatically scales the y-axis of a diagram based on the displayed results.

  • ALL: Scales the y-axis in all windows for an ideal viewing experience.

  • DEFault: Restores the default scale of the y-axis.

  • ONCE: Scales the y-axis in a specific window for an ideal viewing experience.
