Display:Window:Subwindow:Zoom:Area#

SCPI Commands

DISPlay[:WINDow<n>][:SUBWindow<w>]:ZOOM:AREA

Direct SCPI Members

class Area_Data

Structure for setting input parameters.

double X1

Diagram coordinates in % of the complete diagram that define the zoom area. The lower left corner is the origin of coordinate system. The upper right corner is the end point of the system.

double Y1

Diagram coordinates in % of the complete diagram that define the zoom area. The lower left corner is the origin of coordinate system. The upper right corner is the end point of the system.

double X2

Diagram coordinates in % of the complete diagram that define the zoom area. The lower left corner is the origin of coordinate system. The upper right corner is the end point of the system.
