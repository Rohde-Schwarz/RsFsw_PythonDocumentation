Selects the trigger condition for external power triggers.

  • POSitive: The measurement starts in case the trigger signal shows a positive edge.

  • NEGative: The measurement starts in case the trigger signal shows a negative edge.

powerMeter Repeated capability selector

When you do not use any explicit repeated capability selector, the value is taken from its parent group.
