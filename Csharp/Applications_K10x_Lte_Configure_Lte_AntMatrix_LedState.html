Queries the state of one of the instruments in a MIMO setup.

  • GREEN: Connection to the instrument has been successfully established.

  • GREY: Instrument connection has been turned off with CONFigure[:LTE]:ANTMatrix:STATein.

  • RED: Connection to the instrument could not be established.

instrument Repeated capability selector

When you do not use any explicit repeated capability selector, the value is taken from its parent group.
