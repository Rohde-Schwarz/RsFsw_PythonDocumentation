Selects the frequency coupling for power sensor measurements.

  • CENTer: Couples the frequency to the center frequency of the analyzer

  • MARKer1: Couples the frequency to the position of marker 1

  • OFF: Switches the frequency coupling off

powerMeter Repeated capability selector

When you do not use any explicit repeated capability selector, the value is taken from its parent group.
