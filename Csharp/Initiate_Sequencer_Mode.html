SCPI Commands

INITiate:SEQuencer:MODE

Direct SCPI Members

void Set ( SequencerModeEnum mode )#
// SCPI: INITiate:SEQuencer:MODE
driver.Initiate.Sequencer.Mode.Set(SequencerModeEnum.CDEFined);

Defines the capture mode for the entire measurement sequence and all measurement groups and channels it contains. Note: To synchronize to the end of a measurement sequence using *OPC, *OPC? or *WAI, use SINGle Sequencer mode.

  • SINGle: Each measurement group is started one after the other in the order of definition. All measurement channels in a group are started simultaneously and performed once. After all measurements are completed, the next group is started. After the last group, the measurement sequence is finished.

  • CONTinuous: Each measurement group is started one after the other in the order of definition. All measurement channels in a group are started simultaneously and performed once. After all measurements are completed, the next group is started. After the last group, the measurement sequence restarts with the first one and continues until it is stopped explicitly.
