Turns gated measurements on and off. For measurements with an external trigger gate, the measured values are recorded as long as the gate is opened. During a sweep the gate can be opened and closed several times. The synchronization mechanisms with *OPC, *OPC? and *WAI remain completely unaffected. See “(Measurement) Points”.

state ON | OFF | 0 | 1

  • OFF | 0: Switches the function off

  • ON | 1: Switches the function on
