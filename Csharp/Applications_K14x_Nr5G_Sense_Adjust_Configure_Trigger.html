Defines the behavior of a triggered measurement when adjusting a setting automatically (using SENS:ADJ:LEV ON, for example) . See “Adjusting settings automatically during triggered measurements”.

state ON | OFF | 0 | 1

  • ON | 1: (default:) The measurement for adjustment waits for the next trigger.

  • OFF | 0: The measurement for adjustment is performed without waiting for a trigger (corresponds to Continue in manual operation) .
