Selects the channel bandwidth(s) of the carriers in MC ACLR measurements.

  • B510: First carrier: 5 MHz, second carrier: 10 MHz bandwidth.

  • B520: First carrier: 5 MHz, second carrier: 20 MHz bandwidth.

  • B1010: First carrier: 10 MHz, second carrier: 10 MHz bandwidth.

  • B1015: First carrier: 10 MHz, second carrier: 15 MHz bandwidth.

  • B1020: First carrier: 10 MHz, second carrier: 20 MHz bandwidth.

  • B1515: First carrier: 15 MHz, second carrier: 15 MHz bandwidth.

  • B1520: First carrier: 15 MHz, second carrier: 20 MHz bandwidth.

  • B2020: First carrier: 20 MHz, second carrier: 20 MHz bandwidth.

  • USER: Custom combination of bandwidths. Define the bandwidths of both carriers with CONFigure[:LTE]:UL[:CCcc]:BW.
