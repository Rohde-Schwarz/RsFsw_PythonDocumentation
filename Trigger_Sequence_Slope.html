# TRIGger[:SEQuence]:SLOPe
# get:

# set:
driver.trigger.sequence.slope.set(slope = enums.SlopeType.NEGative)

For external and time domain trigger sources, you can define whether triggering occurs when the signal rises to the trigger level or falls down to it.

Parameters:

slope – POSitive | NEGative

  • POSitive: Triggers when the signal rises to the trigger level (rising edge) .

  • NEGative: Triggers when the signal drops to the trigger level (falling edge) .
